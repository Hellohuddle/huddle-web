## Also corrected, not on the list and at no charge

**A display fault of ours.** Depending on how a visitor scrolled, several
sections were staying blank instead of appearing, including the walkthrough, the
floor plans, the backyard comparison, the rotating views and the exterior color
options. Measured on the live site, 37 of 90 blocks could stay invisible on a
normal scroll and 46 of 90 after navigating by the menu. This is fixed on both
pages and verified: nothing stays hidden under fast scrolling, menu jumps,
keyboard navigation or a jump straight to the foot of the page.

**Large screens.** The stylesheet had no rules above 1024 pixels wide, so on a
large monitor the layout kept stretching: text columns ran 1729 pixels wide and
some notes reached 277 characters a line. Content is now bounded and centred,
and type scales up, so the site holds together on an office display or a
television. This also addresses your point about making the embedded viewer
resize without stretching.
