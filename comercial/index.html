Site plan

How the block
is laid out

  • Two wings around a courtyard. The suites meet at the corner, so every front looks onto the same open space.
  • Covered walkway and patio seating along both fronts, with planting between the tables and the drive.
  • Fountain plaza with lounge seating at the open end of the courtyard.
  • Parking wrapping the block, with the accessible stalls and the crossing right at the plaza.
  • Monument sign at the entrance, facing the arrival drive.

The interiors and the names on the storefronts are shown to illustrate how the suites can be fitted out. Suite sizes, layouts and terms are issued on request.
