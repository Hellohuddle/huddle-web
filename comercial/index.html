Commercial Space for Lease

Huddle’s commercial microdistrict is designed to bring distinctive food and beverage experiences, wellness concepts and complementary lifestyle retail together in one welcoming place. With approximately 9,000 square feet of commercial space alongside a neighborhood of 48 townhomes, the vision is a thoughtfully selected mix of businesses that gives residents and visitors reasons to return, from a morning coffee or a wellness appointment to dinner with friends.

We’re welcoming restaurant, café, beverage, wellness and lifestyle businesses that share that vision. Outdoor gathering spaces and a connected setting create opportunities for businesses to become part of everyday life at Huddle.
