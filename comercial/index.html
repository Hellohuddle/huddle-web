The suites turn their fronts onto a brick courtyard: sail shades over the walkway, planting between the tables and a fountain with lounge seating where the two buildings meet.

The suites

Seven suites,
two buildings

From 732 to 1,500 square feet, around the same courtyard. Pick any suite on the plan to see its area, its lease dimensions and the services that reach it.

  • Two buildings around a courtyard. Building 1 holds four suites, building 2 holds three, and every front looks onto the same open space.
  • Covered walkway and patio seating along both fronts, with planting between the tables and the drive.
  • Fountain plaza with lounge seating at the open end of the courtyard.
  • Parking wrapping the block, with the accessible stalls and the crossing right at the plaza.

Areas, lease dimensions and services are taken from the lease suite plans by Powell Studio Architecture, 06/29/26. The outlines drawn over the plan are a guide, not a measurement, and the interiors and storefront names are illustrative. Final terms on request.
